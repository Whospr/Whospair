# Whospair — Privacy Policy

Whospair collects nothing. No server receives your data, so there is no
one to analyse it or hand it to a third party.

## What the app never does
Profiling, selling data, advertising, tracking code, crash reports sent
anywhere, reading your address book, location.

No third-party company receives anything, neither data nor usage signals:
the app only talks to the Tor network, to your contacts, and to its update
distributor.

## What stays on your phone, encrypted
Your keys, contacts, messages and files, until they expire, you delete
them, or after 10 wrong PINs. Photos you send are stripped of their
metadata (location, device model). Uninstalling the app erases everything.

## What others can still see
Your network operator can see that this phone uses Tor, but not with whom
or what. Your contact sees what you send them, and your profile photo if
you allow it.

## Camera
The screen shield (optional) uses the front camera. Everything happens on
the phone: no image is kept or sent anywhere.

## Updates
While the app is open, it regularly asks the update distributor, over Tor,
whether a newer version exists (at most every 30 minutes). This request
contains only its version number: no identity, no contacts, no messages.
Updates can also come from your confirmed contacts, over Tor.

Why so often? Because every new version becomes mandatory 5 days after it
arrives: an up-to-date version refuses to communicate with an older one.
This is deliberate: an old version may contain a flaw. Without updating,
you could no longer reach your friends or colleagues; only the update still
gets through to you.

Whospair is constantly evolving, hence frequent updates. A flaw can be
discovered and exploited before anyone even notices. Fixing quickly keeps
your data out of the wrong hands.

## No recovery
Your identity is a cryptographic key, not an account. There is no password
reset, no backup to restore and no support that could access your data.
